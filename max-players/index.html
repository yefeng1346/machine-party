Party size / quick answer

How many players can play Machine Party?

The launch-build answer is two to four players overall. Four is the confirmed online ceiling; the separate local controller limit is still waiting for the announced couch co-op update.

Launch build
  • Online: 2–4 players
  • Local maximum: not confirmed
  • Mod expansion: unsupported

Short answer: max four players online.

Machine Party is documented as a 2–4 player game. That confirms the supported overall session range, but it does not yet prove that four people can share one PC through local co-op.

Use the online number for a room you can play today. Treat local and modded counts as unconfirmed until official notes say otherwise.

Online

How many people can join a room?

Plan for two to four players in a private online room. If a fifth friend wants to watch, use voice chat or a stream rather than assuming the lobby accepts another player. The multiplayer guide explains room IDs and Steam invites.

Local play

Is four-player couch co-op confirmed?

No. The game has a late-August local co-op target, but the final local player and controller limit has not been documented. Do not read “2–4 players” as a guarantee of four people on one PC, and check the patch notes before buying extra controllers for a couch session. See the local co-op and split-screen guide for the current checklist.

Mods

Can a mod add more players?

There is no supported mod that expands the player cap. A community experiment may exist later, but an unofficial player-count patch would need to change lobby, networking, and minigame logic together. Until the developers document a supported extension, treat any “8-player” claim as unverified and avoid using it in a live account or save.
