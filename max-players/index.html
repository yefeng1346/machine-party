Party size / quick answer

How many players can play Machine Party?

Machine Party supports two to four players in an online private room. Four is the confirmed maximum; the local or couch co-op controller limit and split-screen layout are still not documented.

Current v1.5 build
  • Online: 2–4 players
  • Local maximum: not confirmed
  • Mod expansion: unsupported

Short answer: the maximum is four players online.

Machine Party is documented as a 2–4 player game. That confirms the supported online session range, but it does not prove that four people can share one PC through local co-op or split screen.

Player limits at a glance

Use the online number for a room you can play today. Treat local and modded counts as unconfirmed until official notes say otherwise.

Online

How many players can join a Machine Party room?

Plan for two to four players in a private online room. A fifth player cannot be treated as supported by the current listing; use voice chat or a stream for anyone watching from outside the lobby. The multiplayer guide explains room IDs and Steam invites.

Local play

How many players can play on one PC?

That number is not confirmed. The latest developer note says local co-op is still in development and expected in the next few weeks, but the final local player and controller limit has not been documented. Do not read “2–4 players” as a guarantee of four people on one PC, and check the patch notes before buying extra controllers for a couch session. See the local co-op and split-screen guide for the current checklist.

Beyond four

Can five or more players join, or can a mod expand the cap?

There is no supported mod that expands the player cap. A community experiment may exist later, but an unofficial player-count patch would need to change lobby, networking, and minigame logic together. Until the developers document a supported extension, treat any “5-player” or “8-player” claim as unverified and avoid using it in a live account or save.
