Machine Party FAQ: Max Players, Local Co-op & Crossplay

Machine Party supports two to four players online and native local co-op for up to four players in v2.0+. This FAQ covers player count, couch setup, split-screen behavior, crossplay, matchmaking, controllers, and platform questions.

Quick answer: Machine Party supports 2–4 online players and up to 4 local players.

For one-PC play, open Local, choose Original, Custom, or Arcade, and join with up to four controllers. A group of four can use three controllers plus keyboard and mouse. Cross-platform play is still not documented. Use the max players guide for the full limit, or jump to the local co-op setup.

Player questions

Need steps rather than a short answer? Follow the link inside the relevant card.

How many players can play?

Machine Party supports two to four players online and up to four players in Local mode from v2.0 onward. See the player-limit guide for local and online details.

Is there a single-player mode or bots?

No bot-filled single-player mode is advertised in the current Steam feature list. The official feature list describes a 2–4 player experience.

Does it have local co-op or couch co-op?

Yes, from v2.0 onward. Open Local and choose Original, Custom, or Arcade. The mode supports up to four controllers, or three controllers plus one keyboard-and-mouse player. See the local co-op guide for the full setup.

Is there split screen?

Sometimes. Screen behavior varies by simulation: some use split screen, some use a shared or distance-based camera, and Chisel Gauntlet can switch between one screen and split screen. The local co-op guide lists the documented cases.

Can I use a controller?

Yes, the Steam store lists controller support. Individual trials can use different inputs, so follow the on-screen prompts. Use the controller troubleshooting guide if a pad is missing or mapped twice.

Does it have public matchmaking?

No in-game public matchmaking or lobby list is supported. Use a private room ID or Steam friend invite, or find players through the official Looking For Group Discord.

How do I invite a Steam friend?

Create a room, open the Steam overlay, and use the available invite action. If it is missing, send the lobby’s numeric room ID instead; the invite guide has the recovery steps.

What is Arcade mode?

Version 1.5 adds a quick session of 10 random simulations with random rounds and no intro or outro segments. It samples the existing roster rather than adding new minigames; see the minigames list.

How many minigames are there?

Fifteen. The minigame archive currently has 14 verified official titles and one smoking trial whose official name is still being checked.

Is there a Mario Party-style board?

No board-game layer is advertised. Machine Party focuses on playing the minigames as a collection or jumping into selected favorites.

How long is a session?

Early player reviews report that trying all 15 games once takes roughly an hour. A custom selection can be much shorter.

Which platforms and crossplay options are supported?

Machine Party launched on Steam for Windows and SteamOS/Linux. Cross-platform play with another store or console is not documented, and no console release has been announced on the official store page. See the Steam Deck and Linux guide for handheld caveats.

Is Machine Party Available on PS5, Xbox, or Console?

Not currently. Machine Party is available on Steam for Windows and SteamOS/Linux; no PS5 or Xbox release has been announced.

Does it run on Steam Deck?

The Linux build and controller support make play possible in principle, but treat device-specific performance as early launch territory. Read the current Steam Deck and Linux guide before relying on it for a party.

Who made the game?

Mike Klubnika and GDeavid developed Machine Party. Oro Interactive publishes it, and Alex Peipman composed the industrial soundtrack.

Is it connected to Buckshot Roulette?

It shares a developer and sits in the same broader “Klubnikaverse” conversation, but it is a separate game. You do not need to play Buckshot Roulette first.

Is it connected to Tartarus Engine?

The launch ending uses KETAS, preset, simulation, and SENTINEL language that strongly supports a connection. The precise timeline and machine identity remain fan theory. Read the spoiler-heavy lore notes.

Why will my room ID not work?

The host may have left, recreated the room, or be on a different build. Copy a fresh ID, restart Steam, and follow the online room checklist.
