Site record / plain English

Privacy & local data

Machine Party Wiki does not require an account or ask you to submit personal information. This page explains the limited data involved when you use the site.

Quick summary
  • No account or profile
  • No contact form
  • Achievement checks stay local

Information you provide

There are no user accounts, comments, contact forms, or newsletter forms on this site. Machine Party Wiki does not ask for your name, email address, or social profile.

Achievement checklist

If you use the checklist on the achievements page, your checked items are saved in your browser using local storage under machine-party-achievement-progress. The list is not sent to Machine Party Wiki. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data.

Hosting and security logs

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may process standard request and security information such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp, and error details to deliver and protect the site.

Advertising and analytics

Machine Party Wiki displays third-party native advertising supplied by Adsterra on guide pages. Adsterra and its delivery partners may process standard request and device information and may use cookies, local storage, or tracking pixels for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency control, and measurement. Advertisements link to third-party websites. See Adsterra's cookie policy for more information.

The site does not currently run a separate audience analytics service. An advertising-network verification meta tag may be present in the page source, but that tag does not run code or store data by itself.

External links

Links to Steam, Reddit, developer pages, and other sources take you to third-party websites. Those services apply their own privacy terms after you leave Machine Party Wiki.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the site begins using a new data feature or third-party service. Last reviewed: August 3, 2026.

Anonymous project

This fan resource is published under the name Machine Party Wiki Editorial Team. It does not publish a personal author identity or private contact information.
