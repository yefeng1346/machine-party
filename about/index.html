About this wiki

Machine Party Wiki is an unofficial English-language guide built to answer the question a player has right now—not to bury it under repeated marketing copy.

Project principles
  • Official sources first
  • Player reports labeled
  • Theories separated

Why it exists

Answers before filler

Machine Party launched with short briefings, changing control schemes, private online rooms, 15 achievements, and a community trying to document all 15 lethal trials at once. The useful information was scattered across Steam announcements, achievement descriptions, discussion threads, trailers, and in-game observation. This site organizes those fragments around specific player needs: learning a rule, inviting friends, fixing a controller, understanding local-play support, or decoding the ending.

The site is written for players who would rather get a direct answer and return to the game. A page may be short when the answer is short. It becomes longer only when setup steps, evidence, caveats, or troubleshooting branches genuinely help.

Who publishes it

Machine Party Wiki Editorial Team

The byline used across this site identifies the people maintaining this independent guide collection. It is not the developer, publisher, Steam, or an official Machine Party support channel. Editorial responsibility includes checking sources, labeling uncertainty, correcting outdated launch information, and keeping navigation usable as new guides are added.

For details about evidence standards, corrections, and AI-assisted production, read the editorial policy. The main references are listed on the source desk.
