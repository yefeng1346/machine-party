Achievement checklist

The exact requirement is quoted in plain language from Steam. Tips are conservative setup advice, not guaranteed shortcuts.

Quick draw!

Trial: Firearm Factory

Requirement: Kill someone who assembled a gun before you.

Tip: Finishing second does not end the round. Track the first builder, finish your own weapon, and take the shot before they remove you.

BOOM HEADSHOT!

Trial: Duck Hunt

Requirement: Land a headshot.

Tip: Lead less than you would at long range. Wait for a rival to commit to a predictable path before firing.

This is my hole.

Trial: Tunnel Hazard

Requirement: Win by taking the last nook.

Tip: Do not enter the first safe space you see. Stay close enough to contest the final opening without getting blocked by another subject.

Dodge this!

Trial: Spinebreaker

Requirement: Throw the device with under one second left.

Tip: Attempt this with a stable connection. Aim early, keep the receiver in view, and release only when the timer crosses the final second.

Catch this!

Trial: Debris Platforms

Requirement: Knock someone off by throwing a crate.

Tip: Target a player already near an edge. A clean sideways hit needs less force than trying to move someone across the entire platform.

Just another day at the job.

Trial: The Filter

Requirement: Win a round with 100% accuracy.

Tip: Accuracy matters more than raw speed. Skip panic inputs and follow the displayed filtering rule exactly.

Mind the gap.

Trial: Stable Footing

Requirement: Survive until only 10 tiles remain.

Tip: Preserve an escape route and avoid cutting yourself into an isolated island too early.

Stairmaster

Trial: Wrong Way

Requirement: Take 50 correct steps in a row.

Tip: Treat this as a concentration challenge. Favor a steady rhythm and stop copying another player when their route becomes uncertain.

About those TPS reports.

Trial: Inside Job

Requirement: Infect someone within three seconds of receiving the syringe.

Tip: Stay near the group before roles are assigned. If you receive the syringe, use the nearest reachable target instead of searching for an ideal victim.

Gluttony

Trial: Table Manners

Requirement: Finish eating in under 20 seconds.

Tip: Clean input timing beats frantic mistakes. Keep your hand relaxed and watch the active prompt rather than another player’s plate.

Flourished Memories

Trial: Chisel Gauntlet

Requirement: Survive until the maximum number of cube segments are removed.

Tip: This condition rewards survival and restraint. Make deliberate removals instead of racing every prompt.

Leg it!

Trial: Minefield

Requirement: Reach the end without using sonar.

Tip: Learn a safe sequence in normal rounds first. Do not press the sonar input at any point during the achievement attempt.

Mine. All mine!

Trial: Forklift Certified

Requirement: Have four crates in your deposit area.

Tip: Bank crates early, then defend the fourth delivery rather than driving away for unnecessary cargo.

Will it blend?

Trial: Lethal Rebound

Requirement: Push 15 people into hacksaws.

Tip: This is cumulative. Control the inside line and redirect players who are already moving toward the arena edge.

Full Pack

Trial: Cigarette trial

Requirement: Smoke a total of 20 cigarettes.

Tip: The total is cumulative across rounds. Keep playing the smoking trial; the achievement does not require all 20 in one match.
